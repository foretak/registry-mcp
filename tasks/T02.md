Owner: Sonnet 1

## Goal
Implement the rules engine exactly per `NORBIZ_SPEC.md` inside the shapes from T01.

- `core/rules/common.py` — bodies for `next_weekday`, `next_occurrence`, `roll_forward`. Country-neutral only. No Norwegian constants here.
- `registries/no/rules.py` — `validate_orgnr` (MOD11), `ORG_FORMS` mapping, `derive_status`, `deadlines_for(report, today) -> list[Deadline]`.
- `tests/test_rules_common.py`, `tests/test_rules_no.py` — every numbered test in the spec's test list, plus edge cases: orgnr with spaces, 8/10 digits, letters, checksum remainder 10, deadline on Saturday/Sunday, `today` on the deadline day, `today` one day after (rolls to next year), leap year.
